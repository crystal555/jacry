Book Review - Atomic Habits

Jacry 2026-02-13 20:00:00
Categories： Books Tags： book-review self-improvement habits productivity

“Atomic Habits” by James Clear is a practical guide to building good habits and breaking bad ones.

Key Takeaways

  • Small changes compound over time
  • Focus on systems, not goals
  • Make good habits obvious, attractive, easy, and satisfying

Rating: 5/5

This book changed how I approach personal development. Highly recommended for anyone looking to improve their daily routines.
